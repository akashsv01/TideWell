## Scope

Landing page (Persuade) and the signed-in app (Operate) share one world. App screens inherit it at lower volume.

## Audience and job

A parent in the year after birth, often awake at night with a baby. On the landing page: understand what Tidewell does and start. In the app: a one-minute check-in, then read the trend and act.

## Constraints (user-pinned, 2026-10-18)

Night-Feed Picture Book direction, as sophisticated editorial illustration, never childish cartoon. Palette: warm ivory, deep forest green, muted golden yellow, restrained terracotta. Original gouache illustrations of quiet nighttime care; subtle moon or tide animation. Health information on clean, high-contrast surfaces with readable type, clear actions, direct supportive language. Illustrations add warmth without distracting from check-ins or clinical information. Reduced motion supported.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tidewell is the picture book you are awake inside at 3 a.m.: a quiet room, a window, the moon that pulls the tide. It refuses the wellness-app default of a headline beside a phone mockup over a row of identical feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory paper and deep forest green as the two fields; muted gold is the moon and the one primary action; terracotta appears only in the illustrations (a lamp, a blanket) and never as status. Editorial gouache plates with pigment edges and paper grain; Alegreya set large and upright for headings; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything a tired person must read; health facts on plain paper surfaces with hairline rules, never on illustration.

STORY: The visitor sees a parent feeding a baby by a night window and the moon over a tide line, reads one sentence of what Tidewell does, and starts a check-in. Lower pages show one night in three moments (talk, safety check, morning tide), the risk in one sourced sentence, an annotated sample tide, and four promises, ending on a night page with the same action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An open picture-book spread on one gutter. Left page: the gouache night-feed plate (chair, parent and baby, window, moon rising over the tide line) taking the full page height. Right page, on ivory: "How are you, really?" in large Alegreya, two lines of plain copy, the gold primary action "Start tonight", a quiet "I have an account" and "Try the demo". On phones the plate becomes a short band above the text page.

FORM: Night-Feed Picture Book, position 3 on the ordered list; seed key 6cef850d. Raises kept: status in sentences not badges; literal labels; one mark (moon over tide line) everywhere; type scale tied to urgency; every state addressable with a way back.

SIGNATURE MOTION: Moonrise: on first view the moon rises a few pixels and the tide line breathes once (700 ms, ease-out, then still). In the app the voice button is the moon: its water rises with the voice level while recording. Reduced motion: no movement, the plate is still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
